Insights

What the records show since 2000.

For reference only. SharkGuard shows historical records and general seasonal patterns. It does not predict where sharks are now and does not guarantee your safety. Always follow local beach postings and lifeguard advice.
Incidents: California Department of Fish and Wildlife, Shark Incident Database, 2000 to Feb 2022 (111 confirmed incidents). Seasonal patterns: CDFW White Shark Information and white shark tagging research. Shark paths for white, thresher and salmon sharks replay real satellite-tag tracks (TOPP, Tagging of Pacific Predators, 2002–2013, via the U.S. Animal Telemetry Network); leopard, sevengill and hammerhead paths are illustrative. Not live tracking. Incident locations are approximate.
Relief map: NASA Blue Marble shaded relief and bathymetry (NASA Earth Observatory, via FreeTiler). Emoji: Twemoji, CC BY 4.0. Built with D3, Apache ECharts and three.js. Risk score is provisional (draft v0.4).
